# Arena Beasts - the canon

This file is the source of truth for the world and the 20 Beasts.
Use it for each decision about a model, a move, an effect, a sound or a text.
If a change in the game does not agree with this file, change one of the two. Do not let them drift apart.

All names, places and persons in this file are original.

---

## 1. The world

### Caldmoor
Caldmoor is a harbour city of makers. Twelve guildhalls stand in a ring around the harbour.
Each guild keeps one craft, and each craft keeps one element.

### The Quickening
A work that is finished *truer than its purpose* wakes up. The makers call this the **Quickening**.
It cannot be planned. It occurs when a maker puts a full life into one work, and the work is then needed.
The woken work is a **Beast**.

Each Beast has two things that never change:
- **The heart piece.** The part that woke first. It always glows. It is where the element comes out.
- **The maker's mark.** A small sign that the maker left on the body.

### The Proving
The guilds once settled their disputes with riots. The last riot burned a quarter of the city.
Then the guilds signed the peace under the **Peace Banner**, and they made the **Proving**:
two **Keepers** meet in the arena, each with six Beasts. The winner's guild wins the dispute.

No Beast is destroyed in the Proving. A Beast that falls goes **dormant**, and its Keeper calls it back.
A Beast fights because it wants to show what it was made for. That is why each Beast has one **ultimate**:
the one act that shows its whole story.

### Events that the stories refer to
| Event | What occurred |
|---|---|
| The Night of Still Bells | A fog took the fishing fleet. No bell could be heard. Many ships did not come home. |
| The Guild Riots | The last open fight between the guilds. A quarter of the city burned. |
| The Peace Banner | Twelve crests sewn into one banner. The guilds signed the peace below it. |
| The Kiln Breach | The Great Kiln cracked at night and molten glass ran into the street. |
| The Noon Eclipse | The sun went dark at noon. No sundial had a shadow. |
| The Plague Night | One night in which the apothecaries timed each dose against death. |
| The Quarry Fall | The cliff of the Hexstone quarry came down on a full shift. |

### The twelve guilds
The crest of each guild is the logo of its element in the game.

| Element | Guild | Craft |
|---|---|---|
| Ember | Kilnwrights | Potters, glass-blowers, furnace keepers |
| Tide | Tidewardens | Shipwrights, divers, steam and water engineers |
| Bloom | Greenhands | Gardeners and reef growers |
| Volt | Sparkwrights | Wire drawers and lamp makers |
| Frost | Coldglaziers | Crystal cutters and ice-house keepers |
| Stone | Quarrymen | Masons and sundial makers |
| Gale | Windcallers | Bell founders, organ builders, kite and sail makers |
| Venom | Apothecaries | Healers, poisoners, dyers |
| Mind | Lensgrinders | Clock makers, opticians, mirror makers |
| Shade | Lamplighters | The night watch, lighthouse keepers |
| Iron | Smiths of Anvil Hall | Smiths and founders |
| Brawl | Pitwrights | Dock crews, wrestlers, piston builders |

A Beast with two elements is the work of two guilds, or of one maker who learned two crafts.

## 2. The Beasts

Each entry has the same parts:
**Made** (who, what for) · **Woke** (the Quickening) · **Stands for** (the theme) · **Nature** (how it behaves) ·
**In the arena** (how it fights) · **Ultimate** (what it is and why) · **Look** (rules for the model) · **Ties** (other Beasts).

### 1. Glazefang - the Kiln Door Hound
`kilnhound` · Ember / Stone · Kilnwrights and Quarrymen

- **Made:** Odalys Venn, a kilnwright, made a porcelain mastiff to guard the door of the Great Kiln. She made it in memory of Fang, her dog, who died in a workshop fire after he pulled two apprentices out.
- **Woke:** On the night of the Kiln Breach. The statue stepped into the crack and held it closed with its body. The molten glass ran into its cracks and stayed there.
- **Stands for:** Loyalty. A broken thing that became stronger at the breaks.
- **Nature:** Steady and slow to anger. It does not leave its post. It puts itself between a danger and a person without a thought.
- **In the arena:** It walks forward and does not stop. It makes the floor too hot to stand on.
- **Ultimate - Vesuvian Shatter:** It lets its shell break again, on purpose, as it did that night. Shards fly out, and the molten glass floods the floor. The new shell is heavy, so it becomes slow.
- **Look:** White porcelain with hand-painted cobalt-blue patterns. Orange magma in every crack. An iron grate in the chest with the furnace behind it (heart piece). Steel fangs. A chain collar with a kiln-door key.
- **Maker's mark:** A blue paw print in glaze on the left shoulder.
- **Ties:** Works the Great Kiln with Bellowcroak. Looks up to Forgeshell.

### 2. Mantick - the Striker of the Hours
`pendulummantis` · Iron / Mind · Smiths and Lensgrinders

- **Made:** Ignace Berrow, a clock maker, built it as the striking figure of the Guildhall clock. Each hour it came out of its door and struck the bell. Its wings are cut from the glass of the old clock face.
- **Woke:** On the day that the tower clock was wrong for the first time, by one second. It climbed down from the tower to find the lost second. It still looks for it.
- **Stands for:** Discipline. Exact time. A duty that is done at the correct moment or not at all.
- **Nature:** Strict, formal and impatient with lateness. It counts everything. It bows before and after a fight.
- **In the arena:** Each of its moves is on the beat. Tick is the feint. Tock is the cut.
- **Ultimate - The Twelfth Stroke:** It rings midnight. On the twelfth stroke the day ends and time holds its breath. In that still moment it cuts twice.
- **Look:** Polished brass. A pendulum that swings below the abdomen (heart piece: the glowing pendulum bob). Fore-arms shaped like the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock. Stained-glass wings in amber, ruby and cobalt with lead lines. An escapement wheel that turns in the thorax.
- **Maker's mark:** The numeral XII on the plate between its eyes.
- **Ties:** The rival of Chronovesp, who steals time. Respects Noonshell, the oldest clock.

### 3. Argantler - the Fountain Stag
`mercurystag` · Iron / Tide · Smiths and Tidewardens

- **Made:** Halvard Roon, a smith, and Sunniva Marr, a mirror maker, built it as the centre of the harbour fountain. It was an obsidian stag, and quicksilver ran from its antlers in place of water.
- **Woke:** On a night when a child fell into the harbour. The stag stepped off the fountain. Its antlers became a hook, then a ladder.
- **Stands for:** Change. It becomes the shape that the moment needs. It is also a mirror: you see yourself in it.
- **Nature:** Proud, calm and curious. Its antlers show its mood: smooth when it is calm, sharp when it is angry.
- **In the arena:** It changes range all the time. Lance, scatter, shield: the same silver in a new shape.
- **Ultimate - Tides of Chrome:** It pours out all of its quicksilver as one wave across the arena. Where the wave stops, it sets into spikes. For a moment the stag has no antlers and no guard.
- **Look:** Black volcanic glass with sharp facets. Antlers and hooves of liquid silver that drip and re-form (heart piece: the silver pool at the base of the antlers). Thin silver lines along the flanks, like tide marks.
- **Maker's mark:** A small wave cut into the left haunch.
- **Ties:** Shares the harbour with Atollem. They do not talk much. They do not need to.

### 4. Brasswail - the Fog Organ
`resonancewhale` · Gale / Tide · Windcallers and Tidewardens

- **Made:** Maren Tolliver, an organ builder, built it after the Night of Still Bells. It is a floating organ for the harbour mouth, loud enough to bring a fleet home through any fog.
- **Woke:** The first time it played the roll of the lost ships. Maren had set each ship's name to a chord. At the last chord the whale turned to the sea and answered by itself.
- **Stands for:** Memory. Mourning. A voice that guides you home.
- **Nature:** Gentle, slow and old in its manner. It hums to itself. It does not like silence.
- **In the arena:** It stays far away and moves the opponent with sound and steam. It heals itself with a low note.
- **Ultimate - Symphony of the Deep:** Three movements in one line across the arena: the Call, the Answer and the Homecoming. Each one is wider and louder than the last.
- **Look:** Copper plates with rivets and green patina on the belly. Rows of brass organ pipes on the back (heart piece: the pipes glow from inside when it sings). Steam vents behind the fins. Round fog-lamp eyes. A ship's bell under the chin.
- **Maker's mark:** A tuning fork stamped on the tail fluke.
- **Ties:** Voice, light and needle: Brasswail, Gloomlure and Lodetalon all guide ships. Brasswail does not trust Gloomlure.

### 5. Noonshell - the Courtyard Sundial
`solarisscarab` · Stone / Mind · Quarrymen and Lensgrinders

- **Made:** Tavish Orrin, a mason who studied the sky, carved it as the sundial of the masons' courtyard: a scarab that carries the hour on its back.
- **Woke:** At the Noon Eclipse. The sun went dark and the dial had no shadow. The scarab decided to keep the time itself. The sand in its dome fell upward that day and never came down.
- **Stands for:** Patience. Constancy. Noon: the hour with no shadows, when a thing is seen as it is.
- **Nature:** Slow, stubborn and literal. It speaks seldom. It is never in a hurry and never late.
- **In the arena:** A wall. It waits, and it makes the ground and the air heavy for the opponent.
- **Ultimate - Zenith Inversion:** For one moment it turns the sky over, as on the day of the eclipse. The opponent falls upward, and then the ground comes back. Shots in the air turn round.
- **Look:** Sandstone covered with gold leaf. A quartz dome on the back with sand that floats (heart piece). A sundial blade stands on the dome. Hour lines are cut into the wing cases. Blue lapis inlay on the head.
- **Maker's mark:** A sun disc with one ray, on the head shield.
- **Ties:** The three clocks: Noonshell (the sun), Mantick (the gear), Chronovesp (the sand). It thinks the other two worry too much.

### 6. Coilbell - the First Lamp
`teslajelly` · Volt / Tide · Sparkwrights and Tidewardens

- **Made:** Pim Okafor, a sparkwright apprentice of 14 years, made the first filament lamp in Caldmoor. It failed. She threw it into a tide pool.
- **Woke:** In the tide pool, when Pim looked at it and said "What if...". The copper wires moved. It is the youngest Beast.
- **Stands for:** Curiosity. The first spark of an idea. Joy without fear.
- **Nature:** Playful and easy to distract. It touches everything. It is brave because it does not know the danger.
- **In the arena:** Fast and hard to hit. It stings, moves away and stings again.
- **Ultimate - Brainstorm:** It has all of its ideas at the same time. Lightning falls again and again where the opponent stands, and the floor below Coilbell stays live.
- **Look:** A clear glass bell with a coiled filament that glows (heart piece). A brass screw cap on top, like the base of a lamp. Copper-wire arms with frayed ends and small white ceramic beads. Drops of sea water on the glass.
- **Maker's mark:** A star that Pim scratched on the brass cap with a nail.
- **Ties:** Everyone's small sister. Forgeshell complains about it and guards it.

### 7. Pleatheron - the Thousandth Crane
`origamicrane` · Gale / Iron · Windcallers and Smiths

- **Made:** Ysolde Kaine, a kite maker, folded 999 paper cranes for her brother when he was ill. She folded the last one from one sheet of titanium, with no cut, so that it would last.
- **Woke:** When she closed the last fold. It opened its wings. Her brother got well. Nobody says which came first.
- **Stands for:** A promise that is kept. Hope. Exact work that is also graceful.
- **Nature:** Quiet and elegant. It keeps its word. It does not waste a movement.
- **In the arena:** It glides in, cuts and is gone. Its edges are as thin as paper.
- **Ultimate - Storm of a Thousand Folds:** It opens all of its folds. The 999 paper cranes come with it as a whirlwind of sheets. Inside the storm it heals, and nothing can reach it.
- **Look:** Brushed titanium in flat folded planes with sharp creases. The edges show heat colours: blue, violet and gold. The inner faces are white like paper. A red silk cord is tied round the neck (the promise). The heart piece is the crease at the breast, which glows pale blue.
- **Maker's mark:** A very small kite stamped on the left wing.
- **Ties:** Seamsting cuts and Pleatheron folds. Each thinks the other's method is crude.

### 8. Basaltback - the Quarry Engine
`basaltgorilla` · Stone / Brawl · Quarrymen and Pitwrights

- **Made:** Brannoch Dell, a quarry foreman, and his crew built a lifting engine for the Hexstone quarry. Basalt columns were its weights. Steel pistons were its arms.
- **Woke:** At the Quarry Fall. It held the cliff for a day and a night until all 40 workers were out. The cracks from that day are still on its back.
- **Stands for:** Labour. Strength that serves. Standing together.
- **Nature:** Gentle away from the arena: children climb on it. In the arena it is loud and proud, and it drums on its chest and on the ground.
- **In the arena:** It closes the distance and hits. Each hit shakes the arena.
- **Ultimate - Faultline Overdrive:** Five blows on the ground, like the five horn signals before a blast in the quarry. Each blow reaches farther. The last one brings up the basalt.
- **Look:** Dark grey six-sided basalt columns on the back and the fore-arms. Steel pistons with brass fittings at the shoulders and elbows. Worn yellow warning stripes. A quarry lamp in the chest (heart piece). Orange heat in the old cracks.
- **Maker's mark:** A six-sided stamp in white paint on the right shoulder.
- **Ties:** Trains with Pangyre in the pit. It has never knocked Pangyre down for long.

### 9. Gloomlure - the Last Light of Blackwater
`lanternangler` · Shade / Tide · Lamplighters and Tidewardens

- **Made:** Corwin Ashe, the last keeper of the Blackwater lighthouse, forged it from the iron rail of the lamp room when the lighthouse was closed. The lantern holds the last flame of the old light.
- **Woke:** On a night when wreckers lit a false light to bring a ship onto the rocks. The true flame left the tower and went into the sea after them.
- **Stands for:** The two faces of a light in the dark: a guide and a lure. Hope and temptation.
- **Nature:** Patient and silent. Nobody can read it. It is kind to the lost and has no mercy for liars.
- **In the arena:** It pulls the opponent in with its light and bites.
- **Ultimate - Lighthouse of the Lost:** It puts out every light except its own. In the dark the opponent is drawn to the lantern, and Gloomlure is almost invisible beside it.
- **Look:** Black wrought iron with rust-red edges and rivets. A cage lantern on the lure with a pale blue-green ghost flame (heart piece). The lantern glass has the rings of a lighthouse lens. Short chains, barnacles, needle teeth.
- **Maker's mark:** A lighthouse stamped on the hinge of the jaw.
- **Ties:** The dark mirror of Brasswail. Shutterscale is the only Beast that it cannot fool.

### 10. Pangyre - the One That Gets Up
`gyropangolin` · Iron / Brawl · Smiths and Pitwrights

- **Made:** Dorrit Hale, an instrument maker, built a training figure for the wrestlers of the Pitwrights. A gyroscope in its body made it stand up again after each throw.
- **Woke:** After its ten-thousandth fall. That time it stood up before anyone touched it.
- **Stands for:** Balance. Perseverance. To fall seven times and stand up eight.
- **Nature:** Stubborn, cheerful and competitive. It is small, and it challenges the largest Beast in the room.
- **In the arena:** It rolls, bounces off, and comes back. It does not stay down.
- **Ultimate - Juggernaut Sawblade:** It spins the gyroscope past the red mark and becomes a saw blade. It cuts through the opponent five times. Then it is dizzy and slow.
- **Look:** Bronze scales that overlap, with dark patina at the edges. The gyroscope rings show through the belly, with a blue-white rotor that turns (heart piece). Steel claws. Old leather straps from the pit harness.
- **Maker's mark:** Tally marks in groups of five scratched on the tail scales: its falls.
- **Ties:** Sparring partner of Basaltback. It keeps the score. Basaltback does not.

### 11. Seamsting - the Banner Tailor
`needlescorpion` · Venom / Iron · Apothecaries and Smiths

- **Made:** Evander Lysle, a master tailor, built a sewing engine with scissor claws and a needle tail. The thread is dyed with the strong dyes of the Apothecaries.
- **Woke:** While it sewed the Peace Banner. When it joined the twelfth crest to the others, it understood its work: to mend a thing that is torn, and to pin a thing that tears.
- **Stands for:** Mending and binding. The thread that joins people. The small sting of the truth.
- **Nature:** Fussy and exact, with a dry humour. It cannot leave a loose thread alone.
- **In the arena:** It pins the opponent in position, then cuts.
- **Ultimate - Grand Tapestry:** It sews the floor of the arena into its design, and the opponent with it. The stitches hold the opponent and pull strength out along the thread.
- **Look:** Ivory-white plates with gold seams. Steel scissor claws with a brass pivot screw. A golden needle tail with a glowing violet thread through its eye (heart piece: the bobbin of thread on its back). A red pin cushion with pins on the back. Thimbles on the feet. Tape-measure marks along the tail.
- **Maker's mark:** A cross stitch in gold on the head plate.
- **Ties:** Sewed the banner under which Lustrewing's ballroom was opened. Argues with Pleatheron about cuts and folds.

### 12. Spectrail - the Proof of Light
`prismpeacock` · Mind / Frost · Lensgrinders and Coldglaziers

- **Made:** Aurelian Voss, a lens grinder, built it for the Lens Hall to prove that white light holds all colours. The Coldglaziers cut its 15 feathers from one perfect block of ice crystal.
- **Woke:** In front of a mirror, while it admired itself. Soon after, one feather cracked. It hid for a week. Then it found that the cracked feather makes the best rainbow.
- **Stands for:** Beauty. Vanity, and the cure for it: the flaw is where the colour comes from.
- **Nature:** Dramatic and proud. It loves the crowd. It is still a little shy about the cracked feather.
- **In the arena:** It stays far away and paints the arena with light.
- **Ultimate - Spectrum Convergence:** Seven feathers send seven colours to one point, one after the other. The cracked feather sends the last beam, and the colours become one white pillar.
- **Look:** Polished silver with engraved feather lines. A sapphire crest. 15 glass feathers that float behind it, each with a different tint and an eye spot (heart piece: the eye spots glow). One feather has a crack that is filled with gold.
- **Maker's mark:** An eye spot engraved below the left wing.
- **Ties:** The three lights: Spectrail shows, Shutterscale records, Lustrewing shares.

### 13. Smolderasp - the Keeper of the Still Room
`censerserpent` · Venom / Mind · Apothecaries and Lensgrinders

- **Made:** Mother Sabeline Dray, an apothecary, had it carved from jade as the incense burner of the Still Room. In that room the apothecaries test their remedies and say what they did wrong.
- **Woke:** After a hundred years of smoke and whispered secrets. One night it answered.
- **Stands for:** Secrets. Stillness. That which is hidden, and the mind that fools itself.
- **Nature:** Calm, slow and sly. It never lies. It lets you make your own mistake.
- **In the arena:** It fills the arena with smoke and waits for the opponent to walk into it.
- **Ultimate - Haze of Three Serpents:** The smoke takes its shape. Three serpents come out of the haze. Two are smoke and poison. The opponent must find which one is jade.
- **Look:** Deep green jade segments with pale veins and gold bands between them. A bronze hood with a pattern of small holes, and an ember that glows inside (heart piece). Thin censer chains hang from the hood. Violet and gold smoke. Amber eyes.
- **Maker's mark:** A spiral seal carved on the back of the hood.
- **Ties:** It knows a secret about each other Beast. Shutterscale has a picture of each. They do not like to be in the same room.

### 14. Forgeshell - the Anvil of Anvil Hall
`anviltortoise` · Iron / Ember · Smiths and Kilnwrights

- **Made:** Nobody made it as a Beast. It was the great anvil of Anvil Hall. For 300 years each master smith struck a masterwork on it.
- **Woke:** When the thousandth masterwork was finished on its back. The works woke the anvil. It is the oldest Beast.
- **Stands for:** Endurance. Craft. To take each blow and be better for it.
- **Nature:** Grumpy, wise and slow. It guards apprentices and snaps at poor work.
- **In the arena:** It does not move much. It lets the opponent come, and it hits once, hard.
- **Ultimate - Masterwork Cataclysm:** It takes the blows, as it always did. Each blow heats the shell more. Then it gives back all of them at the same time, in molten iron.
- **Look:** Black cast iron with orange heat in the seams. The shell has the shape of an anvil: a horn at the front and a square hole in the top (heart piece: the glow in that hole). Hammer dents all over the top. A hammer head on the tail. Soot and quench-water stains.
- **Maker's mark:** A brass plate with a hammer and anvil, riveted to the right flank.
- **Ties:** The elder of the Kiln district. Glazefang and Bellowcroak obey it. Coilbell does not, and it permits that.

### 15. Lodetalon - the Way Home
`compasshawk` · Gale / Volt · Windcallers and Sparkwrights

- **Made:** Isaura Trent, a ship's captain, built it from her own astrolabe and compass after she lost a ship. She wanted an instrument that could not lose its way.
- **Woke:** At sea, in a storm that made each compass spin. It flew ahead of the ship, and the ship followed it into harbour.
- **Stands for:** Direction. Purpose. Coming home.
- **Nature:** Restless and sharp-eyed. Loyal to its Keeper. When it rests, it faces north. It has no patience for doubt.
- **In the arena:** The fastest Beast. It picks a line and takes it.
- **Ultimate - True North Descent:** It marks one point on the floor with four beams from the four directions. Then it goes up out of reach and falls on that point, as a compass needle swings home.
- **Look:** Brass astrolabe rings with degree marks make the body. The wing feathers are compass needles: red tips, blue steel shafts. A dark lodestone heart with blue sparks sits in a ring cage (heart piece). The tail is the arc of a sextant.
- **Maker's mark:** An eight-point compass star on the breast ring.
- **Ties:** Flies ahead of Brasswail when a fleet comes in. It will not follow the light of Gloomlure.

### 16. Atollem - the Sea Wall
`coralgolem` · Bloom / Tide · Greenhands and Tidewardens

- **Made:** Old Nerissa Quay, a reef grower, did not build it. She grew it for 60 years on a frame at the harbour wall. The pearl in its chest started as one grain of sand that she put there.
- **Woke:** When a storm surge came at the city. It stood up from the sea wall and broke the wave.
- **Stands for:** Patience. Slow growth. Shelter for small things.
- **Nature:** Gentle and very slow. Small fish and crabs live in it. It does not like haste.
- **In the arena:** It holds its ground, heals and grows back.
- **Ultimate - Living Atoll:** It grows a reef in seconds: the work of 60 years. The reef comes up in a ring round the opponent and holds it. While the opponent stands in it, Atollem heals.
- **Look:** White and pale pink coral with a rough surface. Brain coral on the shoulders. Purple sea fans, anemones and barnacles. A hollow chest with bars of coral, and a blue-green pearl that floats inside (heart piece). Strands of kelp.
- **Maker's mark:** A ring of blue sea glass on its brow.
- **Ties:** Shares the harbour with Argantler. It lets Coilbell sleep in its chest.

### 17. Bellowcroak - the Boiler That Held
`bellowsfrog` · Gale / Ember · Windcallers and Kilnwrights

- **Made:** Fitz Abara, a boiler maker, built the bellows and boiler that feeds air to the Great Kiln. It always ran above the safe pressure.
- **Woke:** On a day when the apprentices forgot the safety valve. It held the pressure in its own body until the workshop was empty. Then it jumped into the harbour and let go.
- **Stands for:** Temper, and the control of it. Pressure that you hold for the sake of others.
- **Nature:** Loud, grumpy and kind. It complains about everything. Its croak is a safety whistle.
- **In the arena:** It pulls and pushes the opponent with air and steam, and it bounces.
- **Ultimate - Overpressure Detonation:** It closes all valves, swells, and at last lets go. The blast throws the opponent across the arena and throws Bellowcroak the other way.
- **Look:** A hammered copper boiler for a belly, with brass bands and rivets. A throat of red-brown leather folds, like an accordion. A pressure gauge on its side with the needle in the red zone (heart piece: the gauge glows). A whistle valve on top. Sight-glass eyes. Soot.
- **Maker's mark:** A red line on the gauge that Fitz painted by hand.
- **Ties:** Works the Great Kiln with Glazefang. Forgeshell tells it to be quiet. It is not quiet.

### 18. Shutterscale - the Honest Witness
`lenschameleon` · Mind / Shade · Lensgrinders and Lamplighters

- **Made:** Liesel Marrow, a lens grinder, built a camera that could walk. Its work was to record each Proving and each guild trial, so that nobody could argue about the result.
- **Woke:** When a guild master told it to lose a plate that showed him cheating. It refused. It hid the plate and itself.
- **Stands for:** Observation. Truth. A memory that cannot be bought.
- **Nature:** Shy and watchful. It does not forget a face. It changes colour when it is embarrassed.
- **In the arena:** It keeps its distance, hides, and takes one clear shot.
- **Ultimate - Freeze Frame:** It takes the picture. The flash catches the opponent in the frame and holds it there. Then the plate develops, and the caught moment burns.
- **Look:** Brass lens barrels with knurled focus rings make the body. Two aperture eyes that move apart from each other (heart piece: the glow in the lenses). A neck of black leather camera bellows. A tail that is a curled film reel. A flash lamp for a crest. Enamel scales that change colour.
- **Maker's mark:** Four small frame corners engraved on its forehead.
- **Ties:** Has a picture of each Beast. Smolderasp knows a secret of each. They watch each other.

### 19. Chronovesp - the Stolen Hour
`hourglasswasp` · Venom / Shade · Apothecaries and Lamplighters

- **Made:** Thaddeus Crane, an apothecary of the night watch, made a dose glass: an hourglass that timed each medicine on the Plague Night.
- **Woke:** In the last minute of that night. The sand ran out, and Thaddeus said "One more hour". The glass took an hour from somewhere. The sand has been dark since then.
- **Stands for:** Urgency. Time that is borrowed. Each second counts.
- **Nature:** Restless and sharp, with a bitter humour. It cannot stay still. It fights as if the time is almost gone.
- **In the arena:** It darts in, stings and drains. It makes the opponent slow and itself fast.
- **Ultimate - Stolen Hour:** It turns the glass over. It takes time from the opponent and gives it to itself: the opponent goes into slow motion with long cooldowns, and Chronovesp is ready again immediately.
- **Look:** A brass thorax with hour marks. The abdomen is an hourglass: two glass bulbs in a cage of brass rods, with dark violet sand and bright magenta grains (heart piece). The sand flows upward when it is excited. A black steel stinger. Wings like thin watch glass.
- **Maker's mark:** A broken minute hand fixed to its back.
- **Ties:** The rival of Mantick: stolen time against exact time. Noonshell tells both to slow down.

### 20. Lustrewing - the Light of the Ballroom
`chandeliermoth` · Ember / Frost · Kilnwrights and Coldglaziers

- **Made:** The twins Oriel and Brisa Fenn made the chandelier of the Guild Ballroom. Oriel, a kilnwright, made the candles. Brisa, a coldglazier, cut the crystal. They had not spoken for years. The work made them talk again.
- **Woke:** At the first Midwinter Ball after the peace. A draught blew out all the candles. The chandelier lit itself again, came down from the ceiling and danced.
- **Stands for:** Welcome. Joy that is shared. Two opposite things in harmony: fire and ice.
- **Nature:** Gracious and social. It loves music and dancing. It is the moth and also the flame, and it knows that this is funny.
- **In the arena:** It stays far away and fills the air with crystal and candle fire.
- **Ultimate - The Last Waltz:** The last dance of the ball. Beams of fire and ice go out from it like the arms of a chandelier and turn round it in waltz time. The opponent must dance with them or burn.
- **Look:** A body of black wrought-iron scrolls with gold leaf. The wings are chandelier arms with rows of crystal drops (clear, with a cyan tint). Candles with warm flames on the antennas and wing tips (heart piece: the candle flames). Wax drips.
- **Maker's mark:** Two linked rings on the thorax, one gold and one silver.
- **Ties:** Its ballroom hangs the Peace Banner that Seamsting sewed. It invites each Beast to dance. Only Pangyre always accepts.

## 3. Rules for new work

1. **Start from the story.** A new move, effect or model detail must agree with the entry of the Beast.
2. **The heart piece glows.** It is the brightest part of the model and the source of the element particles.
3. **The maker's mark is on the model.** It is small, and it is always in the same position.
4. **Three materials.** Each Beast has a main material, a metal and a glow. Do not add a fourth without a reason.
5. **The ultimate is the story.** It shows the moment of the Quickening again, or the thing that the Beast stands for. It has a cost, because that moment had a cost.
6. **A new Beast needs:** a maker with a name, a purpose before it woke, the moment that it woke, one theme, and one tie to a Beast in this file.
